Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026 • Effective Date: November 14, 2026

At TripCrew, we prioritize fair, transparent, and prompt financial handling for all travel groups. This Cancellation & Refund Policy outlines the conditions and timelines under which refunds and cancellations are processed for services and trip pot transactions.

1. Failed or Duplicate Transactions

Standard Refund Window: 5 to 7 Business Days
If your bank account or payment method is debited but the transaction fails to reflect in your Trip Pot balance, or if a duplicate charge occurs due to network latency, the amount is automatically reconciled and returned to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

In the rare event that an auto-refund is not credited within 7 business days, please email us at support@tripcrew.app with your Payment ID (e.g., pay_... from Razorpay) and transaction screenshot. Our finance support team will resolve it within 24 hours.

2. Trip Cancellation by Organizer or Member

  • Pre-Trip Balance Withdrawal: Unspent funds remaining in a Trip Pot can be withdrawn or refunded back to the contributing members at any time prior to the trip start date, subject to the group's mutual agreement.
  • Cancellation Before Expenditure: If a trip is cancelled before any shared expenses (e.g. hotel bookings, vehicle rentals) are made, the trip organizer can initiate a full settlement refund back to members via the app.
  • Post-Expenditure Cancellations: If shared funds have already been utilized for non-refundable external bookings (such as airlines, stays, or tour tickets), refund eligibility depends on the terms of the third-party providers. TripCrew will update the shared ledger to show transparent deduction and remit any remaining balances.

3. Platform Convenience Fees

The 1% platform fee charged on completed pot contributions covers payment gateway processing and cloud telemetry costs. For transactions where a refund is triggered due to gateway errors, system failure, or duplicate deductions, the 1% platform fee is also 100% refunded.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request assistance with any transaction, follow these steps:

  1. Open the TripCrew app and navigate to your Trip → Pay / Pot History.
  2. Select the transaction and tap Report an Issue or note the Transaction Reference ID.
  3. Email us at support@tripcrew.app with your registered phone number, email address, and transaction details.
