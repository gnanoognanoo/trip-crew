Shipping & Delivery / Digital Fulfillment Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026 • Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Digital Goods & Services Notification:
TripCrew provides mobile software services, collaborative itinerary tools, and digital group trip pot fund pooling. We do not sell or ship physical merchandise. All services, access privileges, and wallet/pot credits are delivered electronically and immediately.

1. Delivery Mode & Timelines

All transactions performed through TripCrew are fulfilled digitally through our cloud infrastructure:

  • Trip Pot Fund Contributions: Once your payment is authorized and captured by our payment partner Razorpay, your contributed amount is credited instantly to the corresponding group's digital Trip Pot ledger.
  • App Features & Premium Itinerary Access: Instant activation upon successful authentication or invitation link acceptance.
  • Transaction Confirmation: A digital payment receipt and SMS/email confirmation containing your Razorpay Payment ID is generated immediately upon successful checkout.

2. Shipping Charges

Because all our products and services are 100% digital, there are zero (₹0.00) shipping or courier charges applicable to any user transaction.

3. Service Interruption or Delay in Credit

In rare situations where network connectivity drops during payment redirection, the credit may take up to 15–30 minutes to reflect on your mobile app dashboard as our backend webhooks automatically sync with Razorpay.

If your payment was debited from your bank or card but does not reflect in your trip pot after 30 minutes, please contact us with your payment receipt:
