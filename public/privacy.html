Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026 | Effective Date: November 14, 2026

At TripCrew ("we", "our", or "us", operated by TripCrew Technologies), we value and respect the privacy of every traveler who uses our mobile application and related web services (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, shared, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, profile photo, and mobile phone number provided upon authentication.
  • Trip & Travel Data: Trip destination, dates, starting point, member lists, itinerary stops, checklist tasks, and shared memories.
  • Financial & Payment Transaction Data: When you contribute to a shared trip pot or record expenses, we store records of contribution amounts, payment status, transaction reference IDs, and timestamp. Note: We do not collect or store full credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or bank netbanking passwords. All payment transactions are handled directly through PCI-DSS certified payment aggregator partners (Razorpay).
  • Location Information: Live GPS coordinates are collected only with your explicit permission when you enable "Live Crew Location" or "Trip Pulse" to share real-time location with fellow trip members. You can disable location tracking at any time from your device settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Enable group trip coordination, shared itinerary planning, and expense tracking.
  • Process digital contributions to shared trip pots and verify transactions through Razorpay.
  • Send push notifications for important trip updates, expense settlements, and safety pulses.
  • Provide contextual AI travel recommendations through OpenRouter AI models (default: NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL).
  • Maintain platform security, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable laws and regulatory payment standards.

3. Payment Data Security (Razorpay Integration)

All payment processing for TripCrew Pay is conducted via Razorpay Software Private Limited. Razorpay is a certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant service provider. When you initiate a pot payment, your financial credentials are encrypted and transmitted directly to Razorpay's secure servers. We only receive a transaction confirmation token, payment ID, and payment status.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with:

  • Trip Members: Other travelers in your private trip can view your name, shared expenses, itinerary suggestions, and active location pulses.
  • Service Providers: Cloud infrastructure (Firebase / Supabase), Payment Gateways (Razorpay), and Maps providers (OpenStreetMap / Google Maps) solely to operate the app.
  • Legal Obligations: When required by lawful process, court order, or applicable law enforcement directives.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You retain full ownership of your data. You may request account deletion or removal of trip records at any time by navigating to your in-app Profile settings or emailing us at support@tripcrew.app.
